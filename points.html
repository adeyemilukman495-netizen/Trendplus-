What are Pulse Points?

Pulse Points are experience points. They track your progress through TrendPulse challenges and help build your level, streak and leaderboard status.

They are not money, cannot be withdrawn and do not promise cash rewards. Additional ways to use points will be decided as the rewards system develops.
