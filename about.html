About TrendPulse

A free trends platform that is open about where its numbers come from.

Where the data comes from

Trend numbers come from the public Wikimedia Pageviews API: how many times each English Wikipedia article was read each day. We compare the last 7 days with the 7 days before to find rising topics. Data usually arrives about a day late.

What it means

Page views are a proxy for public curiosity. They are not sales, ratings, streams or total audience size, and news or releases can spike them. Treat them as a signal of attention, not a ranking of quality.

Community features

TrendPulse now uses Supabase for account registration and sign-in, member profiles, shared community posts, watchlists, reviews, direct messages, fan clubs, short-video links and moderation reports. These features require an account. Some other sections, including parts of Battles and Daily Duel, may still use browser-only data or remain in preview mode.

Privacy and safety

Account and community information is stored by Supabase. Public profiles and community content can be visible to other visitors; private watchlists and conversations are restricted to their owners or participants. Media uploaded to the public TrendPulse media bucket may be accessible to anyone with its public link. Do not upload private or sensitive material, and do not share passwords, home addresses, phone numbers or school details in profiles or messages. Read the Privacy Policy before creating an account.

Partnerships and advertising

TrendPulse is designed to support future sponsorships, advertising and premium features. Any paid placement will be clearly labelled as an advertisement.
