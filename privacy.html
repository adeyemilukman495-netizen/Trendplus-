Your data matters

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. What TrendPulse collects

TrendPulse is a static website. It does not currently require an account, ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location, or operate its own user database.

2. Preferences stored on your device

Features may save choices such as followed topics, category preferences, watchlist entries, reviews, community post previews, video links, profile details and message drafts in your browser's local storage. This information stays in that browser on your device and is not sent to a TrendPulse account server. You can clear it by clearing this site's browser storage.

3. Third-party services

TrendPulse requests public trend information from Wikimedia and catalogue information from Kitsu, TVmaze and Wikipedia when you use Explore. Your browser connects directly to these providers, so they may receive standard technical request information such as your IP address. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

4. Images and entertainment information

Anime and manga catalogue results are requested from Kitsu; television and drama results are requested from TVmaze; film discovery searches Wikipedia. Their coverage and fields differ. TrendPulse does not currently verify streaming availability or provide a complete worldwide movie database.

5. Cookies, advertising and analytics

TrendPulse does not currently run its own advertising, analytics or account cookies. Hosting providers and third-party services may use technical logs or technologies under their own policies. If analytics or advertising are added later, this policy will be updated and any required choices will be provided.

6. Children and accounts

TrendPulse is a public information and entertainment discovery website. Do not submit private or sensitive information in public discussions or external services linked from the site.

7. Changes

This policy may change as features are added. The date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact or issue-reporting route provided in the TrendPulse project repository. Do not post personal or sensitive information publicly.
