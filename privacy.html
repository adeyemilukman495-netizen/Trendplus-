Your data matters

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

1. Information used by accounts and community features

TrendPulse offers optional account registration and sign-in through Supabase. When you create an account, Supabase processes your email address and authentication information. TrendPulse does not display or store your password in its own website code. If you use community features, the service may store the profile details you submit (such as display name, username and bio), posts, comments, likes, follows, watchlist entries, reviews, messages, fan-club memberships, short-video links, stories, moderation reports, blocks and notifications.

2. How information is shared

Profile information and community content are readable by other visitors where the feature is designed to be public. Watchlists are restricted to the signed-in owner. Direct messages are restricted by database access rules to members of the conversation. Moderation reports and block records are restricted to the relevant account and authorised backend processes. TrendPulse media uploads use a public storage bucket, so uploaded files may be accessible to anyone who obtains the public file link. Do not upload private, confidential or sensitive material.

3. Browser storage and sessions

The site may store your Supabase sign-in session and local preferences in your browser so you remain signed in and your settings work. Clearing browser storage may sign you out or remove locally saved preferences. Some older preview-only sections may still store their data only in your browser.

4. Hosting and third-party services

TrendPulse is hosted using GitHub Pages and uses Supabase for account, database and media services. These providers may process technical information such as IP address, device/browser details and service logs to operate and secure their services. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies.

5. Third-party catalogue and font services

TrendPulse requests public trend information from Wikimedia and catalogue information from Kitsu, TVmaze and Wikipedia when you use Explore. Your browser connects directly to these providers, so they may receive standard technical request information such as your IP address. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

6. Images and entertainment information

Anime and manga catalogue results are requested from Kitsu; television and drama results are requested from TVmaze; film discovery searches Wikipedia. Their coverage and fields differ. TrendPulse does not currently verify streaming availability or provide a complete worldwide movie database.

7. Cookies, advertising and analytics

TrendPulse does not currently operate its own advertising programme. The site uses first-party, privacy-conscious traffic measurement to count page views and estimate unique visitors. It stores a randomly generated visitor ID in your browser storage and a session ID for the current browser session, and records the page path, referring website hostname when available, and visit time in Supabase. It does not intentionally collect your name, email address, full referring URL, or page content for this analytics feature. Clearing browser storage may reset the visitor count for your browser. Supabase and the hosting provider may also process technical information such as IP address and service logs to operate and secure their services. The website may load Google Fonts and make requests to the catalogue and trend providers described above.

8. Safety and account use

Do not share passwords, verification codes, home addresses, phone numbers, school details, financial information or other sensitive information in profiles, posts or messages. Use the report and block features if you encounter inappropriate behaviour. Do not upload content you do not have permission to share. Anyone who is not legally able to create an account in their location should not register.

9. Changes

This policy may change as features are added. The date at the top will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact or issue-reporting route provided in the TrendPulse project repository. Do not post personal or sensitive information publicly.
